🔍 Allowance Abuse Scanner

What is this?

This tool scans ERC-20 token allowances on BNB Smart Chain (BSC) to identify potentially risky or excessive token permissions granted to other addresses.

Why is this important?

Token allowances allow one address (spender) to transfer tokens on behalf of another address (owner). If allowances are too high or granted to untrusted contracts, your tokens could be at risk.

Risk Levels

  • CRITICAL Infinite or near-infinite allowances that could drain all tokens
  • HIGH Allowances significantly exceeding balance or very large absolute values
  • MEDIUM Allowances exceeding balance or granted to contracts
  • LOW Allowances within reasonable limits

How it works

  1. Enter a wallet address to scan
  2. Optionally add specific spender addresses to check
  3. Click "Scan Allowances" to analyze token permissions
  4. Review the results and risk assessments

Common Use Cases

  • Security audits before connecting to DeFi protocols
  • Checking permissions granted to DEX routers
  • Identifying forgotten or excessive allowances
  • Monitoring token permissions for compliance

Supported Tokens

The scanner checks common BSC tokens including USDT, BUSD, USDC, ETH, BTCB, CAKE, and DAI. You can also specify custom spender addresses to check specific contracts.
